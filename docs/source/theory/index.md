# Concepts

This section explains the mathematics behind the variational quantum algorithms in
Mandacaru: the variational principle, the electronic Hamiltonian and its mapping to
qubits, the UCCSD ansatz, and the adaptive construction of ADAPT-VQE. The guides say
how to run them; these pages say why they work and what they do not guarantee.
